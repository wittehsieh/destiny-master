# Domain Interpretation Recipes
Use these as retrieval guides. They do not replace structural interpretation.

## mindset
- primary: 福德, 命
- secondary: 遷移
- inspect: mental activity, self identity, external feedback
- avoid: equating 福德 with happiness only

## career
- primary: 官祿, 命
- secondary: 財帛, 遷移, 僕役
- inspect: capability, role, authority, recognition, team environment, earning linkage
- avoid: equating 官祿 alone with salary

## finance
- primary: 財帛
- secondary: 官祿, 福德, 田宅
- inspect: earning, management, retention, spending, risk, asset base
- avoid: equating 財帛 with guaranteed wealth

## relationship
- primary: 夫妻
- secondary: 命, 福德, 官祿, 財帛
- inspect: partner expectation, interaction style, emotional needs, work/finance pressure
- avoid: predicting divorce from one star

## health
- primary: 疾厄
- secondary: 命, 福德, 遷移
- inspect: stress, habits, balance, environment
- avoid: medical diagnosis or mortality prediction

## family
- primary: 父母, 子女, 田宅
- secondary: 命, 夫妻
- inspect: support, authority, parenting, home base, intergenerational interaction
- avoid: deterministic family events
